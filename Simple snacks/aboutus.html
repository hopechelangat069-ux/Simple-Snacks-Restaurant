Who We Are

Simple Snacks is a Ugandan restaurant focused on providing affordable, delicious and hygienically prepared food and snacks. Our goal is to create a friendly environment where customers can enjoy quality food and excellent service.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide fresh, affordable and high-quality food while giving every customer a welcoming and satisfying restaurant experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local restaurant known for excellent food, good customer service, cleanliness and affordable prices.

Qualities of a Good Restaurant

1. Cleanliness and Hygiene

A good restaurant should maintain clean kitchens, utensils, tables, toilets and dining areas.

2. High Food Quality

Food should be fresh, properly prepared, tasty and served at appropriate temperatures.

3. Good Customer Service

Employees should be respectful, welcoming, patient and responsive to customers.

4. Affordable Prices

A restaurant should provide value for money while maintaining food quality.

5. Comfortable Environment

Customers should have a clean, safe, attractive and comfortable place to enjoy their meals.

6. Reliable Service

Customers should receive consistent food quality and service whenever they visit.
